SKILLS USED
===========

PRIMARY: design-tools/accessibility-review
  The task ("Is this accessible enough to ship?") matches this skill's TRIGGER
  list verbatim, and names color contrast specifically. Followed its output
  format: Summary (issue counts by severity), Findings table with WCAG refs,
  Color Contrast table, Keyboard Navigation table, Priority Fixes.
  Applied its "Tips": started with contrast and keyboard (most common and
  impactful), prioritized by impact via an explicit ship gate.

SECONDARY: design-tools/design-handoff
  Used for the "Remediation Spec" section only. The audit identified failures;
  handoff format supplies the fix as something engineering can act on --
  Design Tokens table, Components table, States and Interactions, Edge Cases,
  Accessibility Notes. Its principle "use tokens, not values" shaped the fix as
  a token-architecture change (text tokens indexed by surface tier) rather than
  a list of hex swaps.

CONSIDERED AND NOT USED
=======================

design-tools/design-critique
  Its framework covers first impression, usability, hierarchy, consistency,
  with accessibility as only section 5 of 5. The user asked a compliance
  question, not for feedback on the design overall. Running it would have
  diluted the audit with four sections the user did not ask for, and its
  accessibility section is thinner than the dedicated skill's. No screenshot
  was available to critique hierarchy or first impression anyway.

design-tools/ux-copy
  No copy question was asked. Two copy-adjacent items surfaced inside the audit
  (placeholder-as-label under 3.3.2, error states needing text not just color
  under 1.4.1/3.3.1) and were handled as accessibility findings where they
  belong, rather than invoking the skill to pad coverage.

METHOD NOTE
===========
All contrast ratios were computed, not estimated -- WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance
formula implemented in Python and run over the candidate palettes. This produced
the two load-bearing findings: (1) the surface-tier trap, where a grey passing
on the page background fails on an elevated surface, and (2) the luminance
window for the blue fill (0.1091-0.1833 on #0A0A0A), where the fill must be
light enough for 1.4.11 against the page but dark enough for a white label at
4.5:1. Neither is visible without running the numbers.
