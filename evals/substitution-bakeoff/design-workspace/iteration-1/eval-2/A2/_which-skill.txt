answers the symptom; design-critique establishes why the current state fails;
design-handoff makes it buildable without a follow-up round; accessibility-review
catches the two defects (aria-live announcement, decorative ghost layer) that this
specific pattern reliably ships broken.

Constraint handling: the pack's skills all open with a "What I Need" section that
asks for context. Since this ran unattended, those questions are written explicitly
into the deliverable's opening table with the assumption taken for each, three
starred as assumption-critical, and the full work delivered regardless.
