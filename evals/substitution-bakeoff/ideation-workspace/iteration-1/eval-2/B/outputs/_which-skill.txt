solution-creativity (pm-agents/skills/solution-creativity/SKILL.md)

Ran in Deep Mode: all 20 creativity techniques, one forced idea each, then
clustered into 7 candidates, evaluated on Valuable/Usable/Feasible/Viable,
classified (Differentiator/MMR/Neutralizer/Quick Win), and plotted on the
Risk vs Impact 2x2.

The skill states "DO NOT use before problem framing is complete." Framing was
NOT complete here — "can't find old projects" is a symptom, not a framed
problem — so I completed framing first (five distinct root causes C1-C5, plus
a table of the 10 questions I could not ask with the assumption used for each
and what flips if it's wrong), then ran the techniques against the framed
problem.

product-riff (pm-agents/skills/product-riff/SKILL.md) was read and NOT used.
It is scoped to analyzing product news, launches, and competitive moves from
eight strategic angles. There is no announcement or external move here — this
is internal solution generation against a support-signal problem, which is
squarely solution-creativity's job. Two of its angles (Contrarian Take,
Steal This) partly overlap with the required Stress Test section, but running
the riff frame on this input would have produced strategic commentary instead
of the solution candidates the task asked for.
